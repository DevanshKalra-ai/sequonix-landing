Questions

Before you ask

How long does a project take?

Most websites launch in two to four weeks. Automations are often live within days, because the work is mostly in the planning.

How is pricing worked out?

A fixed quote after the discovery call, based on the scope we agree. No hourly billing and no surprises at the end.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. The code, the accounts and the domain are yours from day one. If you ever want to take it elsewhere, you can.

What happens after launch?

Support and monitoring are included. When something breaks, we usually know before you do, and it is fixed before it costs you a client.

Tell us what you're building.

A free discovery call, no slides. We will map how a site and a few automations would change your week, then send a plan and a fixed quote.
